# Design notes

Researched 17 October 2026. The sources below informed the design; their layouts, copy, statistics, and run schedules were not transferred to Lightning.

## Research

- [Lightning by Neesh — official Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/lightningbyneesh/): the source for the existing cobalt, red, and cream identity, community photographs, and announced Lightning events.
- [Neesh Wickremasinghe on community through running — The Morning](https://www.themorning.lk/articles/TgufPVGjrTzKrhkP7uJA), 26 July 2026: a first-person interview supporting the Club story and its short attributed Neesh quote. The emphasis is on making connections and creating a shared community.
- [Bandit — About](https://banditrunning.com/pages/about): named people, local experiences, photographs, and a dated origin story make community tangible. The useful lesson is specificity rather than additional motivational copy.
- [Tracksmith — Trackhouse](https://www.tracksmith.com/au/pages/trackhouse): practical upcoming runs sit within the community page. Dates, event names, and clear actions provide a useful hierarchy.
- [SATISFY — Chamonix Off-Road Supply](https://satisfyrunning.com/pages/chamonix-off-road-supply): explicitly labeled event information keeps a strong campaign identity useful to someone planning to attend.
- [Patta — Community](https://patta.nl/pages/community/) and [Running](https://patta.nl/pages/patta-running): real people, places, and event recaps connect running with an existing culture.
- [FAT Running](https://www.fatrunning.com/): reviewed in the browser. Runs, Shop, The Club, and FAQ establish a compact structure; Home includes upcoming runs with practical event metadata, followed by club information and FAQ. Lightning uses only its own confirmed information.

## Design decisions

These are design interpretations of the research, rather than claims of objective superiority.

- Put actual Lightning people first through a photographic wall. On request, restore the earlier Neesh cutout on a cobalt disc and a compact activity panel. The running illustration is conceptual, and its decorative route is not a GPS track. Activity cards show real announced events with working details.
- Keep the original cobalt, red, and cream palette; use a consistent typographic voice and readable utility labels.
- Keep the full five-event calendar in static HTML on Home. Filters help scanning, while explicit details and registration actions make the next step clear. Calendar and share links serve a practical purpose.
- Make Runs a dedicated page in the main navigation, alongside the complete Home calendar.
- Use shorter copy and a sourced Neesh quote on Club. Let photographs and confirmed events convey the energy.
- Recompose image crops, spacing, headings, and event rows for mobile. Keep motion restrained and preserve usable reduced-motion behavior.

Unconfirmed times, distances, prices, and participation figures are not invented. Official Lightning announcements remain the reference for final event details.
