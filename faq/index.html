Ask the crew
01What is Lightning?

A running and social community started by Neesh in Sri Lanka. We get together for runs, Run & Rave events, and good company.

02New to running. Where do I start?

Message the crew before your first session. Tell us a little about your experience and ask about the run’s distance and pace groups.

03Where and when do you meet?

The calendar lists announced dates and venues. Meeting points and times are shared on Instagram before each event.

04How do I register?

The 25 October Run & Rave has an official registration form. For other dates, check the crew’s latest announcement. Entry details and availability are set by the organisers.

05What should I bring?

Running shoes, water, and anything listed in the event announcement.

06Where can I get Lightning merch?

Browse the official shop, or message the crew about availability.
